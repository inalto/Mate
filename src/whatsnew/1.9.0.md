# What is new in Mate 1.9.0

## Ruler

- **A ruler on the page.** Press **Ruler** in the toolbar, or the `R` key, to show it. Press again to hide it. You can change the key in Settings, Keyboard shortcuts, and "Show or hide the ruler" is also in the command palette. The ruler is a light blue bar across the pane. It is always the same width on screen, whatever the zoom. Each pane of a split view has its own ruler.
- **Move it.** Drag the bar with one finger, the pen or the mouse.
- **Turn it.** Put two fingers on the bar and turn them. You can also drag the round handle near either end, or focus a handle and use the arrow keys (Shift with an arrow turns by 0.1 degrees). With a mouse, hold Alt and use the wheel over the bar (Shift and Alt for 0.1 degrees). A plain wheel over the ruler scrolls the page as usual.
- **Fingers on the bar do not touch the page.** They never pan, zoom or draw. One finger on the bar and one off it do two things at once: the first moves the ruler and the second pans the page.
- **It stays with the page.** Panning and zooming carry the ruler along. If it is off screen, hide it and show it again to bring it back to the middle of the pane.
- **See the angle, or type it.** The angle, from 0 to 180 degrees from the page's horizontal, is shown in the middle of the bar. Tap it, type an angle and press Enter. Escape cancels.
- **Scales.** The ruler shows millimetres on one edge and inches on the other. In Settings, Pen, Ruler, **Scales**, you can show millimetres or inches on both edges. The scale measures the page as an A4 sheet, 210 mm wide, so its centimetres are true when the page is printed scaled to fit an A4 sheet (not at 100 %). The ticks get coarser as you zoom out, and the numbers grow in both directions from a zero mark on the ruler.
- **Not saved, not exported.** The ruler is not part of the page. It is not saved, not an undo step, and not in exports, prints or thumbnails. It is hidden in presentation mode.

## Straight lines

- **Draw along an edge.** With the pen, the fountain pen, the highlighter or the cover tool, start a stroke on either edge of the ruler, or just beside it. The stroke runs straight along that edge for its whole length. Pressure still changes the width. A small label next to the pen shows the length.
- **Erase along an edge.** The eraser follows the edge the same way.
- **The ruler stays put.** While you draw such a stroke the ruler does not move, even if your hand rests on it.
- **An ordinary stroke.** The result is a normal stroke. Scribble to erase, ink to shape, perfect shapes and straight-lines mode leave it alone.
- **Round lengths.** In Settings, **Snap line length to** can make a line end on a multiple of 1 mm, 5 mm, 1 cm, 1/8 in or 1/4 in. It is off by default.

## Snapping

- **Angle.** The ruler snaps every 15 degrees when it is within 2 degrees of one. In Settings, **Snap the angle every** can be Off, 1, 5, 15 or 45 degrees. Hold Shift while dragging a handle to turn it off.
- **Page lines and objects.** When the ruler is horizontal or vertical, an edge snaps to the lines of the page if **Snap to grid** is on, and to the edges and centres of other objects if **Smart guides** is on. A guide line shows what it matched. Hold Shift while dragging to turn it off.

## Page-turner pedals

- **PageDown and PageUp turn pages.** PageDown alone goes to the next page and PageUp alone to the previous page. Ctrl+PageDown and Ctrl+PageUp still work.
- **Bluetooth pedals.** Set the pedal to its Page Up / Page Down mode. Its Space / Enter mode cannot be used.
- **Hold it, one page.** Holding the pedal down turns only one page.
- **Other keys.** You can assign other keys in Settings, Keyboard shortcuts, Pages.

## Toolbar

- **Undo and Redo are always visible.** They sit at the end of the bar and never scroll away.
- **Tools are regrouped.** The select tools now sit next to the pens.
- **One Insert button.** Image, media, table, code, equation and graph are now in a single **Insert** menu. Those six buttons moved there. You can put any of them back on the bar with Customize toolbar.
- **More.** The **More** button holds the tools that do not fit or that you hid, **Customize toolbar** and the toolbar position. The choices of the old "Move toolbar" button are there now.
- **Customize toolbar.** Show or hide a button, change the order with the arrow buttons or by dragging the handle, and add separators. You can open it from More or from Settings, Appearance.
- **A second row.** If you want more buttons in view, turn on **Use two rows**. A second row leaves less room for the page.
- **Reset to default.** One button puts the toolbar back the way it was.
- **Saved per device.** Your layout belongs to the device you set it on.
- **A mark for buttons with options.** A small mark in the corner shows which buttons have options. Press the active tool again to open them.
- **Remove pen.** The pen menu now has a **Remove pen** button. Right-clicking the pen still works.

## Fixes

- **Connector curves.** The connector tool in curve mode now bows when neither end is attached to an object. It used to draw a straight line.
- **Graphs.** A graph keeps its proportions when you resize it by a corner.
- **Covers.** A cover drawn with perfect shapes on now follows its outline, like the highlighter.
- **Covers menu.** The screen is no longer left dimmed when you remove the last cover while the covers menu is open.

## Languages

- The new buttons and messages are translated into German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese. These translations are machine translations that no speaker has reviewed yet.
