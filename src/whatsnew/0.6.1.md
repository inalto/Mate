# What is new in Mate 0.6.1

## Graphs

- Type functions the way you would on a calculator: `sin(x)`, `sqrt(x)`, `abs(x)`, `ln(x)`, `log(x)`, `exp(-x^2)`, `pi`, `theta`, `x^10`. LaTeX still works.
- Implicit curves: equations in x and y such as `x^2+y^2=4`, `x^2/9+y^2/4=1` or `y^2=x^3-x` are drawn too.
- Under each expression, Mate shows how it read it, so a typo is visible at once.
- `f(x) = …` and `r(θ) = …` are accepted.

## Fixes

- The recent pages list on Home keeps its spacing between the title and the date.
- Thumbnails show up when Mate runs against a custom data folder (`MATE_DATA_DIR`, for developers).
