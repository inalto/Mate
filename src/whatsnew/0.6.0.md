# What is new in Mate 0.6

## More AI providers, and a clearer setup

- Choose from Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, Z.ai, Moonshot Kimi, xAI, Together, or run a model on your own computer with Ollama or LM Studio. A custom OpenAI-compatible address works too.
- Settings, Intelligence now starts with "What Mate needs from an AI": checking handwriting needs a model that can read images; asking, summaries and practice only need text. Each provider card says what it needs, whether it can check handwriting, what it costs and where to get a key.
- Fetch models lists the models your key can use. Test connection checks a local server is running.
- Local providers send nothing to the internet. The permission text says so, and changes if you point Mate at a remote address.

## Your language

- Mate speaks English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese and Chinese. It follows your system language; change it under Settings, General, Language.
- English and Italian are reviewed. The others are machine-translated first passes and say so in the picker. Corrections are welcome on GitHub.

## Share a book

- Share… in the book menu saves a copy of the book into a synced folder Mate finds on your computer (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud Drive or Nextcloud), or any folder you pick, and shows it in the folder.
- With a Nextcloud account (Settings, Sharing), Mate uploads the book and gives you a public link, with an expiry.
- `.mate` files now open in Mate: double-click one and it is imported as a new book.

## Maths engines

- Select a typed equation and press Compute: Evaluate, Simplify, Expand, Solve or Numeric value. The result appears under the equation. Everything runs on your computer.
- The tutor card has Check locally: Mate tests the equation itself, without any AI, and says whether both sides agree, for which values it holds, or that it never holds.
- With a free Wolfram|Alpha AppID (Settings, Maths engines), Ask Wolfram|Alpha returns a short answer.

## New look

- A new logo, in the app icon, the About dialog and the welcome screen. Mate is your maths mate, and in Italian "mate" is what students call matematica.
