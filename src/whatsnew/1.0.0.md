# What is new in Mate 1.0.0

## Line ends

- A line or arrow can now have an arrow, a dot or a bar at either end. When a stroke snaps to a line or arrow, a small **Line ends** panel opens next to its end point: pick Start and End from None, Arrow, Dot or Bar. It closes when you touch the canvas, press Escape, or after 5 seconds.
- Select a single line or arrow and choose **Line ends** in the selection bar to change the ends later. Each change is one undo step.
- Older arrows keep their arrow head. The ends show the same on screen, in PNG, SVG and PDF exports and in thumbnails.
- Do not want the panel? Turn off **Offer line ends after snapping** under Settings, Writing.

## Toolbar docking

- The toolbar can sit at the top, bottom, left or right of the page. Use **Move toolbar** at the end of the bar, or Settings, Appearance. On phones and small tablets only top and bottom are offered.
- The bar fills the edge, scrolls when it does not fit, and the page area shrinks beside it, so it never covers your ink.
- Menus open away from the edge they are docked to.

## Paper in the toolbar

- The **Paper** button moved from the top bar into the toolbar, with the page templates and sizes in a panel next to it.

## SVG, audio and video

- **Insert media** adds mp3, m4a, wav, ogg, mp4 or webm files to a page, up to 256 MB each. You can also drop or paste the files.
- Audio and video play inside the page with their own controls. To move or resize a player, use the select tool: with the cursor tool the player takes your clicks, and a thin strip along its top edge selects it. Video keeps a 480 by 270 box and shows the picture letterboxed inside it.
- Exports (PNG, SVG, PDF) and thumbnails show a placeholder with a play symbol and the file name. Media travels inside `.mate` archives.
- SVG images without a size now insert using their view box, and pasting SVG text from a browser or editor inserts it as an image.

## Dictation

- The **Dictate** button records your voice and turns it into a text box at the centre of the view, or adds the text to the text box you are editing. Press it again to stop. Recordings stop after 5 minutes.
- Choose a speech service under Settings, Dictation: OpenAI, Groq, Mistral, a speech server on your own computer or network, or any compatible address. Keys are kept in the system keychain.
- Mate sends the recording to the service you chose only after you allow it. If you answer Not now, it asks again the next time you dictate. Nothing is kept by Mate.

## Known limitations

- Dictation and media playback have not been tried on every platform yet. Microphone access on Linux and iPad is untested, and on macOS the app asks for the microphone but a signed build may need an extra entitlement.
- Media players are not part of PNG, SVG or PDF exports; a placeholder is drawn instead.
- Dock positions, media playback through the asset protocol and the microphone prompts still need checking on real devices.
- The new text is translated in English and Italian by hand; the other languages are machine translated.
