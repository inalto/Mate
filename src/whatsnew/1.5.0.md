# What is new in Mate 1.5.0

## Shapes

- **Reshape by the corners.** Select a rectangle, triangle or any other polygon and drag the round handle on a corner: only that corner moves.
- **Circles and ellipses.** A circle or ellipse has four round handles. Drag one to stretch it; a stretched circle becomes an ellipse.
- **Curves.** A curve made by holding the pen still has three handles: its two ends and its middle. Drag the middle to bend it.
- **Resize keeping the form.** The square handles of a selected shape now stand a little off it and resize it without changing its proportions. Hold **Shift** to stretch it freely.
- **Edit with the tool you draw with.** With **Perfect shapes** on, or with the shape tool, tap the outline of a shape to select it, then drag its handles. The handle below the shape moves it. Press anywhere else to go on drawing.
- **Hold, then adjust.** When holding the pen still turns a stroke into a shape, keep the pen down and move it: the shape grows, shrinks and turns until you lift.
- **Perfect shapes keeps your pen.** Turning **Perfect shapes** on leaves the pen you are writing with selected, and picks the pen when another tool was in hand.

## Pages

- **Double-tap to fit.** On a paper page, tap twice with one finger to make the sheet span the window, whether it was larger or smaller. Tap twice again to see the whole sheet.
- **Line and grid spacing for new pages.** **Settings, Paper** has a new slider for the size of the squares, lines or dots on new pages.
- **Copies land below.** What you paste on another page, or send with **Send to another page**, goes on a row of its own under what is already there, so the next copy lands below it.

## Library

- **Name a notebook as you create it.** A new notebook opens its title for typing at once.

## Trackpad

- **Pinch to zoom.** Pinching on a trackpad zooms the page on Windows and macOS.
