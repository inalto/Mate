# What is new in Mate 1.6.0

## Graphs

- **Square graphs.** A new graph is square, like its view from −10 to 10 on both axes.
- **Choose the proportions.** The graph panel has a new **Proportions** choice. **Keep the box** leaves the graph as it is, whatever the view. **Same scale on both axes** gives the graph the shape of its view when you finish editing, so one unit is as long across as it is up.
- **Pinch to zoom.** While editing a graph on a touch screen, pinch with two fingers to zoom in and out, and move them together to pan.
- **The panel stays on screen.** The panel for entering functions goes to the right of the graph, or over it, when there is no room on the left, and moves up when it would run past the bottom.

## Shapes

- **Fill perfect shapes.** A new option in **Settings, Pen** fills closed shapes drawn with **Perfect shapes** with a lighter tint of the ink colour.
- **Perfect shapes with the highlighter.** With **Perfect shapes** on, the highlighter draws clean lines, boxes and circles too.
