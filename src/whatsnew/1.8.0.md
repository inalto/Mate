# What is new in Mate 1.8.0

## Cover and recall

- **Hide it, then remember it.** Pick the new **Cover** tool (key `O`) and draw over a word or drag out a box. The cover hides what is under it, whatever the colour of the page. Choose stroke or box in the tool's preset menu, and set the default colour and width in Settings.
- **Add it to your toolbar.** If you used Mate before, the toolbar has no cover pen yet: add one from the **+** pen menu. Until then, `O` gives you a temporary one.
- **Tap to peek.** Tap a cover and it fades so you can read what is under it; tap again to hide it. Peeking never changes your page: it is not saved, not an undo step, and every page opens fully covered. The **Covers** menu can reveal all, cover all, or remove all covers in one undo step (locked covers stay).

## Test me

- **Walk the covers.** **Test me** in the Covers menu goes through the covers in reading order and scrolls to each one. Reveal it, then press **Knew it** or **Missed**. At the end you see your score and your time, and can repeat just the ones you missed.
- **Keys.** Space reveals, 1 or the right arrow is Knew it, 2 or the left arrow is Missed, and Escape ends the test.

## Statistics

- **See how you are doing.** **Recall** in the library shows, for a notebook, how many covers you have, how many answers you gave, how many you knew in the last 7 and 30 days, and for every page its last score, its number of tests and a trend. Press **Test** on a page to open it and start a run.

## Exports and backups

- **Covers stay covered in exports.** PDF, images, print, copy as image and thumbnails all draw covers covered. In the export dialog choose **Covered** or **Left out** to export the page without them. Typed text under a cover is also left out of a PDF's searchable text and of LaTeX exports.
- **Your scores stay with you.** Answers are never part of an export or a shared file. Backups carry them, and **Restore from backup** brings them back.

## Languages

- The new buttons and messages are translated into German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese.
