# What is new in Mate 1.11.0

## Export

- **A section or a whole book as one PDF.** In **Export**, with **PDF** chosen, **Include** offers **This page**, **This section** or **Whole book**. Every page goes into one file, in the order of the sidebar.
- **Table of contents.** The PDF lists the sections and the page titles. Tap a title in a PDF reader to jump to that page.
- **For other note apps.** GoodNotes, Notability and Samsung Notes open the PDF, and you can write on top of it there. What you wrote in Mate arrives as part of the page: it cannot be erased or moved in the other app.
- **Covers stay.** Covers are kept covered on every page unless you choose **Left out**.

## Languages

- The new buttons and messages are translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese. These translations are machine translations that no speaker has reviewed yet.
