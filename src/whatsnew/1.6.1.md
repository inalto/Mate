# What is new in Mate 1.6.1

## Pages

- **The page stays at the edge of the screen.** When a page is zoomed in wider than the screen, it now moves sideways only until its edge reaches the edge of the screen, so no empty margin shows beside it. A page narrower than the screen stays centred.
- **On for new installs.** This is the **Keep the page centred horizontally** option in **Settings, Gestures**. It is now on by default. If you installed Mate before, turn it on there; turn it off to reach the margin beside the page again.
