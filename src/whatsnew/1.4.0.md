# What is new in Mate 1.4.0

## Writing

- **No more Copy menu while writing.** On iPad, tapping twice quickly with the pen no longer brings up the system Copy menu in the middle of a stroke.
- **Ink stops at the edge of the sheet.** A stroke that leaves the sheet now ends where it crosses the edge, instead of drawing a line along the border.
- **Lines snap to horizontal and vertical.** A straight line drawn within a few degrees of horizontal or vertical lands exactly on it.
- **Move the end while you hold.** When holding the pen still turns a stroke into a line or arrow, keep the pen down and move it: the end follows until you lift.
- **Edit lines afterwards.** Select a line or arrow and drag the round handles on its ends. Hold **Ctrl/Cmd** to place an end freely.
- **Curves.** A long, smooth bend becomes a clean curve the same way a line does: hold the pen still at the end.
- **Perfect shapes.** The new **Perfect shapes** toggle in the toolbar (**Shift+S**) tidies every stroke that reads as a shape, line, arrow or curve as soon as you lift the pen.
- **Fill closed ink.** A pen stroke that closes on itself can be filled: select it and use the fill menu, as for shapes.

## Touch

- **Double-tap to zoom.** Tap twice with one finger to zoom in on that spot.
- **Two-finger double-tap to undo.** Tap twice with two fingers to undo the last action.
- **Keep the page centred.** A new option keeps a page that is narrower than the window centred horizontally.

These three are in **Settings, Gestures**.

## Selection

- **Send to another page.** The selection bar has a new button that sends the selection to any other page: pick the page, then **Copy there** or **Move there**.
- **The selection bar stays on screen.** It no longer slides off the edge of the window or wraps out of view.
- **Handles go away.** Pressing on the page after editing a graph, table, code block or text box deselects it.

## Colours

- **Your own swatches.** In the pen colour picker and the fill menu, **+** saves a colour as a swatch and **−** lets you remove swatches.
