# What is new in Mate 0.8.0

## Touch

- A finger now draws by default. The old "Draw with finger" switch is replaced by **Finger action** (Settings → Gestures & touch): Automatic, Draw or Pan. Automatic draws with a finger, and the fingers pan while you are using a pen.
- Two fingers pan and zoom.
- On tablets the toolbar has a finger button that cycles Automatic, Draw and Pan.
- A fingertip is no longer rejected as a palm.
- Hold-to-shape works with a finger as well as the pen: hold still at the end of a stroke (750 ms by default, under Settings → Pen & stylus → Ink to shape) and a line is straightened or a shape recognised. A finger gets a little more room to wobble while it holds.

## Paper

- Six new templates: Isometric, Hex, Cornell, Handwriting, Columns and Music.
- A **Paper** button in the editor top bar opens a picker with a preview of each template, spacing presets (Small, Medium, Large) and **Use for new pages**.

## Android

- The launcher icon is now the Mate logo.

## Known limitations

- Touch drawing and the pen behaviour have been tested on the Android emulator, not yet on real tablets with a pen.
- The new template names are not translated yet outside English and Italian.
