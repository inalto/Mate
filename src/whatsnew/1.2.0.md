# What is new in Mate 1.2.0

## Whiteboard tools

- **Connectors.** Draw a line between two things and it stays attached: move a box and the connector follows. Choose straight, curved or elbow routing, an arrow, dot, bar or diamond at either end, dashed or solid, and add a label that you can slide along the line. Press **C** or use the Connector button.
- **Text inside shapes.** Double-click a shape, or select it and start typing, to write inside it. The text wraps to fit and you can change its colour, size and alignment.
- **Auto-complete arrows.** Select a shape, text box or note and four small arrows appear around it. Click one to add a connected shape, text or note in that direction, or drag it onto something to connect the two.
- **Sticky notes.** The Note button (**Shift+N**) adds a coloured note. Any text box can become a card: pick a colour, corner radius, shadow and border from the selection bar.
- **Frames.** Press **F** to draw a frame, or pick a ready-made size (slide 16:9, slide 4:3, square, A4). Frames have a title, can have a background, and carry what is inside them when you move or duplicate them. Export a single frame from its menu.

## Presenting

- **Presentation mode.** Start it from the Present button or with **Ctrl+Shift+P**. Mate steps through your frames one at a time, with next and previous buttons, arrow keys, full screen and a black background outside the frame. Choose **Fit** to see the whole frame or **Fill** to cover the screen. Reorder the frames by dragging them in the frame order list.

## Mind maps

- Press **M** or use the Mind map button to start a map. **Tab** adds a topic below the current one, **Enter** adds one beside it, arrow keys move around, **F2** edits and **Delete** removes a topic.
- Choose one of four styles and a layout (left, right or balanced), set the colour of a branch, and fold a branch away to keep the map tidy. Drag a topic onto another to move it with everything under it.
- Import a FreeMind (`.mm`) or OPML file from the Mind map menu.

## Selecting and arranging

- **Rotate** anything with the round handle above the selection. Hold Shift to turn in 15 degree steps.
- **Lock** things you do not want to touch by accident (**Ctrl+Shift+L**). Locked items cannot be moved, changed, erased or selected with the lasso, and a copy of a locked item is unlocked.
- **Smart guides** line up what you move or resize with the edges, centres and spacing of other items. Hold Ctrl (Cmd) to suspend them, or turn them off under Settings, Paper.
- Hold **Alt** while dragging to drag a copy. Hold **Shift** while moving to keep to one direction.
- **Fit page** (**Shift+1**) and **Zoom to selection** (**Shift+2**), also in the menu on the zoom level.
- **Export selection only**, and **Copy as image** from the selection bar to paste into another app.

## Languages

- The new tools and settings are translated into German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese.
