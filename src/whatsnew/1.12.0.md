# What is new in Mate 1.12.0

## Function study

- **A full-page editor.** Editing a graph now opens it on the whole screen: the functions on the left, the graph in the middle and the study on the right. On a phone these are three tabs. **Done**, the close button or Esc returns to the page.
- **Functions in pieces.** A function can have a different formula on each part of its domain. Press **Add piece**, then give each piece its interval. Leave an end empty for infinity, and choose whether each end is included. On the graph an included end is a full dot and an excluded end is an empty dot.
- **Restrict the domain.** A function with one formula can be limited to an interval in the same way.
- **Study.** For the function you select, Mate shows the domain, the intersections with the axes, the zeros, the sign, the limits and asymptotes, the first derivative with the intervals where the function rises and falls, the maxima and minima, the second derivative with the concavity and the inflection points, and the integral and area between two values.
- **Show on the graph.** Each part of the study has a switch that draws it on the graph: points, dashed asymptotes, the curve of the derivative and the shaded area. They stay on the page and in exports.
- **In the visible range.** The study is calculated with numbers on the part of the graph you see. Move or zoom the graph to study another part. Very narrow details can be missed.
- **Symbols.** A row of buttons above the formula types powers, roots, π and absolute value.

## Selection

- **The rotation handle is free.** The menu above a selection now sits above the rotation handle. Before, the menu covered the handle.
- **The lasso outline stays.** After you select with the lasso, the outline you drew stays visible and follows the selection when you move it.
- **A handle to move.** A selection has a round handle with four arrows below it. Drag the handle to move the selection. Dragging inside the selection still works.
- **Small selections.** For a very small selection the handle is a crosshair on its right side, and pressing inside the selection moves it. Before, a press inside a small selection resized it.

## Pages

- **The zoom stays when you turn the page.** Turning the page with a swipe, PageDown, PageUp or a pedal keeps your zoom level and shows the top of the new page. Opening a page from the sidebar still fits it to the width.

## Connectors

- **Cleaner curves.** A curved connector now takes one of three shapes from the sides it leaves: a **C** when the sides are at right angles, a **U** when both ends leave on the same side, and an **S** when they leave on opposite sides. The curve goes around the two objects.

## Send to another page

- **Go to origin.** Something you sent to another page or notebook remembers where it came from. Select it and press **Go to origin** to open that page with the original in view. If the original was moved or deleted, Mate opens its page.

## Languages

- The new buttons and messages are translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese. These translations are machine translations that no speaker has reviewed yet.
