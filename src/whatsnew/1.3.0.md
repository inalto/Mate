# What is new in Mate 1.3.0

## Record a lecture, replay your notes

- **Record while you write.** Press the Record button in the toolbar and write as usual. A red time shows in the top bar of every pane; press it to pause, resume or stop. Recordings can run for up to four hours, and Mate warns you five minutes before the end.
- **A marker on the page.** When you stop, a small round marker with the length of the recording appears at the top-left of what you were looking at. Press it to open the replay bar at the bottom of the pane.
- **See the notes as they were written.** While you replay, everything written after the playhead is dimmed, and it comes back to full strength as the audio reaches it. Tap any note and the audio jumps to just before you wrote it.
- **It follows your pages.** If you turned pages while recording, the replay turns them with you. Switch **Follow pages** off to stay where you are. Speed goes from 0.75 to 2 times, and Space, the left and right arrows and Escape control the bar from the keyboard.
- **Safe when things go wrong.** If Mate goes to the background (on an iPad, when you switch app), the recording pauses, a banner tells you how much was missed, and the gap is marked on the scrubber. If the app closes unexpectedly, the recording is recovered the next time you start Mate.
- **One recording at a time.** Dictation is unavailable while you record, and leaving the notebook asks whether to stop the recording first.

## Transcript

- Press **Transcript** in the replay bar and then **Transcribe** to get the words with their times, through the speech service you chose for dictation. Nothing is sent until you allow it. Tap a sentence to jump there, copy the text, or insert it on the page as a text box. If one part fails, retry just that part.

## Sharing and backups

- **Recordings stay private by default.** Exporting a book or sharing it leaves the audio out unless you tick **Include recordings**, because a lecture holds other people's voices.
- **Backups always carry the audio**, and **Settings, Backup, Restore from backup** adds the notebooks of a backup (recordings included) beside the ones you have. Nothing is replaced or deleted.

## Languages

- The new buttons and messages are translated into German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese.
