# What is new in Mate 1.3.0

## Writing

- **Ink stays on the sheet.** In page mode, strokes now slide along the edge of the sheet instead of running off it, and a stroke that starts off the sheet does nothing. Items you place or move keep inside the width of the sheet.
- **Straight lines.** The new **Straight lines** toggle in the toolbar (**Shift+L**) makes every pen, highlighter and fountain-pen stroke a straight segment. Hold **Shift** while drawing to make just one stroke straight.

## Touch

- **Pinch right after the pen.** Pinch to zoom and two-finger pan now work straight after you have used the pen. Before, touches were ignored for two seconds.
- **Momentum panning.** One-finger panning keeps moving with momentum after you lift your finger.

## Export

- **Annotations for imported PDFs.** When a page contains imported PDF pages, choose **Annotations: None / Flattened / Separate layer**. The separate layer (PDF only) is an optional content layer named "Mate annotations" that PDF editors can hide or edit.
