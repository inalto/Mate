# What is new in Mate 1.10.0

## Photo scan

- **Scan a photo.** Press **Scan** in the toolbar, or choose **Scan a photo** in the command palette. A photo of a sheet of paper, a whiteboard or a book page becomes a flat, straight image on the page.
- **Take or choose a photo.** On iPad and iPhone, **Take photo** opens the camera and **Choose photo** opens the photo library or Files. On the desktop, **From file** or **From webcam**.
- **Corners.** Mate finds the four corners of the page by itself. Drag any corner that is wrong. A magnifier shows the spot under your finger. A focused corner also moves with the arrow keys, and Shift with an arrow takes bigger steps. If no page is found, the corners start as a rectangle for you to place.
- **Rotate and reset.** **Rotate** turns a sideways photo. **Reset corners** starts over.
- **Clean-up.** Choose **Colour**, **Grey**, or **Black and white**. Black and white whitens the paper and removes shadows. A small preview shows the result.
- **Straighten.** Select an image already on the page and press **Straighten** in the selection bar to flatten it in place. **Undo** brings the photo back.
- **Stays on the device.** Everything happens on the device. The photo is not sent anywhere.

## Tabs

- **Other books stay open.** Opening a page of another book now opens it in a new tab. The book you were in keeps its tab, so you can switch back from the tab row. Moving between pages of the same book still uses the same tab.
- **Tabs show their book.** When pages of two or more books are open, each tab starts with the name of its book and a dot in the book's colour.
- **The active tab is always in view.** On a narrow screen the tab row scrolls to the tab you opened or picked.
- **Recording.** Opening another book while you record no longer asks to stop the recording, because the recorded book stays open in its tab. Closing its last tab still asks.

## Pages in the sidebar

- **Page actions by touch.** Each page in the sidebar has a **Page actions** button that opens its menu. A right-click still works.
- **New page above or below.** The menu can add a page just above or just below the page you chose.
- **Clear page.** **Clear page** removes everything written or placed on a page. Locked items and imported PDF pages stay. Mate asks first. If the page is open you can undo it. If the page is not open it cannot be undone.
- **Drag to reorder.** Drag a page up or down among its neighbours. With a finger or the pen, hold the page for a moment, then drag. A quick swipe still scrolls the list.

## Page-turner pedals

- **PageDown and PageUp turn pages.** PageDown alone goes to the next page and PageUp alone to the previous page. Ctrl+PageDown and Ctrl+PageUp still work.
- **Bluetooth pedals.** Set the pedal to its Page Up / Page Down mode. Its Space / Enter mode cannot be used.
- **Hold it, one page.** Holding the pedal down turns only one page.
- **Other keys.** You can assign other keys in Settings, Keyboard shortcuts, Pages.

## Toolbar

- **Undo and Redo are always visible.** They sit at the end of the bar and never scroll away.
- **A long bar scrolls.** When the bar is longer than the window, it scrolls, and a fade at the edge shows that there is more.
- **Tools are regrouped.** The select tools now sit next to the pens.
- **One Insert button.** Image, media, table, code, equation and graph are now in a single **Insert** menu. Those six buttons moved there. You can put any of them back on the bar with Customize toolbar.
- **More tools.** The **More tools** button holds the tools you hid, **Customize toolbar** and the toolbar position. The choices of the old "Move toolbar" button are there now.
- **Customize toolbar.** Show or hide a button, change the order with the arrow buttons or by dragging the handle, and add separators. You can open it from More tools or from Settings, Appearance, Theme.
- **A second row.** If you want more buttons in view, turn on **Use two rows**. A second row leaves less room for the page.
- **Reset to default.** One button puts the toolbar back the way it was.
- **Saved per device.** Your layout belongs to the device you set it on.
- **Zoom buttons on iPad and phone.** On iPad and phone the zoom buttons are hidden by default. You can show them with Customize toolbar.
- **A mark for buttons with options.** A small mark in the corner shows which buttons have options. Press the active tool again to open them.
- **Remove pen.** The pen menu now has a **Remove pen** button. Right-clicking the pen still works.

## Fixes

- **Connector curves.** The connector tool in curve mode now bows when neither end is attached to an object. It used to draw a straight line.
- **Graphs.** A graph keeps its proportions when you resize it by a corner.
- **Covers.** A cover drawn with perfect shapes on now follows its outline, like the highlighter.
- **Covers menu.** The confirmation for **Remove all covers** now appears above the page. Before, the toolbar could turn dark and stop responding, with no dialog to answer.
- **Fingers after the pen.** Right after writing with the pen, fingers can pan the page and use gestures at once. Before, they were ignored for two seconds.
- **Highlighter shapes.** A shape drawn with the highlighter while perfect shapes is on now follows its outline, corners included. A square used to come out with its corners cut.
- **Undo after a perfect shape.** Undo now takes back a stroke that was turned into a shape as its own step.
- **Leftover handles.** Selection handles no longer stay on the page after you deselect.

## Languages

- The new buttons and messages are translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese. These translations are machine translations that no speaker has reviewed yet.
