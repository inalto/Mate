# What is new in Mate 1.10.0

## Photo scan

- **Scan a photo.** Press **Scan** in the toolbar, or choose **Scan a photo** in the command palette. A photo of a sheet of paper, a whiteboard or a book page becomes a flat, straight image on the page.
- **Take or choose a photo.** On iPad and iPhone, **Take photo** opens the camera and **Choose photo** opens the photo library or Files. On the desktop, **From file** or **From webcam**.
- **Corners.** Mate finds the four corners of the page by itself. Drag any corner that is wrong. A magnifier shows the spot under your finger. A focused corner also moves with the arrow keys, and Shift with an arrow takes bigger steps. If no page is found, the corners start as a rectangle for you to place.
- **Rotate and reset.** **Rotate** turns a sideways photo. **Reset corners** starts over.
- **Clean-up.** Choose **Colour**, **Grey**, or **Black and white**. Black and white whitens the paper and removes shadows. A small preview shows the result.
- **Straighten.** Select an image already on the page and press **Straighten** in the selection bar to flatten it in place. **Undo** brings the photo back.
- **Stays on the device.** Everything happens on the device. The photo is not sent anywhere.

## Languages

- The new buttons and messages are translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese. These translations are machine translations that no speaker has reviewed yet.
