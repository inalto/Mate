# What is new in Mate 1.8.1

## Look and feel

- **Controls follow your theme.** Sliders, checkboxes, selected text and scrollbars now use the colours of the theme you chose instead of the system blue.
- **Buttons show what they are doing.** A button that cannot be used is now dimmed, and a menu item that cannot be used no longer lights up under the pointer. Buttons and toolbar buttons darken while you press them.
- **Clearer in dark themes.** The shadows under bars and menus are now visible in the dark, sepia and high contrast themes, and a dark colour swatch keeps a visible edge on a dark menu.
- **Less motion if you ask for it.** The short fade on buttons is turned off when your system is set to reduce motion.
